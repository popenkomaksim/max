Suggested shortlist

  1. Shape-coded badge (30) as the steady state. It reads at a glance, survives colour-blindness and green terrain, and leaves the standard fields free for what they mean.
  2. Time-since label (5) for stale and offline only. An online sensor needs no text. A silent one needs the answer to "since when", and field G is a standard slot for it.
  3. Pulsing halo (15) once per received ping, not continuously. A single pulse confirms a live link without a map full of permanent motion.

Limits to check first

  • The standard's bar colours describe capability. Yellow means Damaged and red means Destroyed, so using them for link state changes their meaning for anyone trained on APP-6D.
  • The app's map canvas does not draw the condition bar for non-mine symbols today. Variants 1 and 6 need the SVG path or new canvas code.
  • Colour-only variants fail for red-green colour-blind users and lose contrast on green map tiles.
  • Continuous motion needs a redraw every frame on an OpenLayers canvas. Cost grows with the number of sensors on screen.
